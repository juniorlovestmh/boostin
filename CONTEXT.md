# Boostin Domain Context

Boostin helps one person learn from their own professional content without
turning a social network into an automation target or a private-data warehouse.

## Vocabulary

- **post**: content authored by the local user, including their commentary on a
  repost.
- **analytics snapshot**: metrics exported by the platform for one post at one
  capture time.
- **profile snapshot**: manually recorded aggregate profile metrics.
- **private review**: a local report that can include post text and notes.
- **public review**: an allowlisted report containing aggregates and explicitly
  approved excerpts only.
- **outcome**: a user-entered count or note about a recruiter, peer, or client
  conversation; always labeled self-reported.
- **real import**: any archive or workbook not marked as a synthetic fixture.

## Non-goals

Scraping, browser control, auto-posting, automated comments or reactions,
connection automation, outreach, CRM functionality, and causal growth claims
are permanently outside the v1 boundary.
